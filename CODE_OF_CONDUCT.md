# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LibreCoding is a place for young people to learn to program. Everyone who takes
part — in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space — is
expected to help keep it welcoming, patient and safe.

This applies regardless of age, experience level, gender, gender identity,
sexual orientation, disability, appearance, neurotype, nationality, ethnicity,
religion or the programming language someone happens to like.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be patient.** Many participants are beginners, and many are children or
  teenagers. "It's obvious" is never a reason to be dismissive.
- **Explain, don't mock.** Questions are the point of a learning platform.
- **Give feedback on the code, not the person.**
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share anyone's personal information, and do not
  share your own (real name, school, address, phone number, photos).
- **Keep it appropriate for a young audience.** Assume a 10-year-old may read
  anything you write.
- **Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution**, and ask for the reason
  instead of arguing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, mockery, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory or sexual comments or imagery of any kind.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission (doxxing).
- Grooming, or any attempt to move a conversation with a minor to a private,
  unmoderated channel.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated products.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer
privately at <https://github.com/Reaccionador2Jeje>.

Reports about the safety of a minor are treated as the highest priority. Reports
are handled confidentially, and retaliation against someone who reports a
problem is itself a violation of this code of conduct.

## Consequences

Depending on severity, the maintainer may:

1. ask for a change in behaviour and explain why;
2. edit or delete a comment, issue or pull request;
3. issue a temporary ban from the project spaces;
4. permanently ban the person from the project.
